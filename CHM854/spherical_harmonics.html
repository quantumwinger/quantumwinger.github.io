Energy levels & ladder operators

📖 Key Equations

$$\hat H = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2I}\Lambda^2 = \frac{\hat L^2}{2I},\qquad I = mr^2$$
$$E_\ell = \frac{\hbar^2}{2I}\,\ell(\ell+1),\quad \ell=0,1,2,\dots,\quad m_\ell=-\ell,\dots,\ell$$
$$Y_{\ell m_\ell}(\theta,\phi) = N_{\ell m_\ell}\,P_\ell^{|m_\ell|}(\cos\theta)\,e^{i m_\ell\phi}$$
$$\hat L^2 Y_{\ell m_\ell} = \hbar^2\ell(\ell+1)\,Y_{\ell m_\ell},\qquad \hat L_z Y_{\ell m_\ell} = m_\ell\hbar\,Y_{\ell m_\ell}$$
$$\Psi(\theta,\phi,t) = Y_{\ell m_\ell}(\theta,\phi)\,e^{-iE_\ell t/\hbar}$$
$$\hat L_\pm Y_{\ell m_\ell} = \hbar\sqrt{\ell(\ell+1)-m_\ell(m_\ell\pm1)}\;Y_{\ell,m_\ell\pm1}$$

🔍 Things to Explore

  • Stationary states: With time evolution on, the phase moves but $|Y|$ (the shape) never changes. Only $|\Psi|^2$ is observable, and it is time independent.
  • Direction of circulation: For complex $Y_{\ell m}$ with $m_\ell>0$, lines of constant phase move toward $+\phi$ (counter-clockwise seen from $+z$); flip to $m_\ell<0$ and they reverse. Compare with the $\pm m_\ell$ states of the particle on a ring.
  • $\ell = 0$: $E=0$, so nothing evolves at all. There is no zero-point energy on a sphere.
  • Real vs complex: Real orbitals ($p_x$, $d_{xy}$, …) are combinations of $\pm m_\ell$. They have the same energy but are not eigenfunctions of $\hat L_z$, so $\langle L_z\rangle = 0$ and they show nodal planes through the $z$ axis.
  • Nodes: Every $Y_{\ell m}$ has $\ell$ angular nodes. In real form: $\ell-|m_\ell|$ nodal cones (constant $\theta$) and $|m_\ell|$ nodal planes (constant $\phi$).
  • Vector model: $|L_z| < |\vec L|$ always, so $\vec L$ never points exactly along $z$. $L_x$ and $L_y$ are undetermined, which is why $\vec L$ is drawn somewhere on a cone.
  • Ladder operators: $\hat L_\pm$ keep $\ell$ (and $E$) fixed and step $m_\ell$ by $\pm1$; the ladder stops at $m_\ell=\pm\ell$.
