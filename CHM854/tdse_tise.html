🔎 Things to Explore

  • Keep mixing at 0% and watch the left plot. Re[Ψ] and Im[Ψ] both oscillate — yet |Ψ|² on the right never moves. The phase e−iEt/ℏ just rotates Ψ without changing its size. Can you explain why the square of a pure phase is always 1?
  • Slowly drag the mixing slider toward 50%. At exactly what mixing value does the probability density start to oscillate? What is special about 0% and 100%?
  • At 50% mixing with kₐ=π and kₑ=2π, watch the right plot. Does the probability appear to shift from one side of the box to the other? What term in |Ψ|² is responsible?
  • Increase the gap between kₐ and kₑ (try kₐ=π, kₑ=4π). Does the oscillation speed up or slow down? How does a larger Δk relate to a larger ΔE and a shorter period?
