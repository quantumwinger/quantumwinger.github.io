🔎 Things to Explore

  • Increase k and decrease k. What happens to the wavelength λ and the momentum p? Can you find the relationship between them?
  • Make the packet very narrow (small σ). What happens to the oscillations inside? What does this tell you about the momentum of a spatially confined particle?
  • Watch the envelope (dashed curve on the right plot) and the wiggles inside it. Do they move at the same speed? Which one represents the particle’s velocity?
  • Look at the curvature of Re[ψ] when k is large vs. small. The kinetic energy is proportional to −∂²ψ/∂x². Can you see why higher k means higher KE?
